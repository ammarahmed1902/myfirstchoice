These external URLs are retained rather than claiming that the photos were downloaded or verified in this environment. The cloud network denied access to images.unsplash.com during development, so browser validation here exercises the local fallback path. Verify the external photographs in the deployed browser and replace unsuitable imagery with approved catalogue photographs. No stock, model, performance, certification or affiliation claims are inferred from a photograph.

Original local SVG illustrations in `public/images/` are used only as automatic fallbacks. External photograph failures do not leave broken image boxes. Fonts are served locally from installed Fontsource packages.
