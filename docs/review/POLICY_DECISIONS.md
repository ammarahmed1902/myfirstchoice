## Returns: unpublished 15-day clause

The business mentioned 15 days, and subsequently answered “when user order” about its start. This appears to suggest the order date, but is not enough to publish an enforceable deadline.

Confirm all of the following before drafting the clause:

1. Does 15 days mean reporting damage, dispatching a physical return, or the company receiving a return?
2. Does the clock start on the order date, dispatch date or receipt date? Confirm the interpretation of “when user order”.
3. How is a parcel that has not arrived reported? An undelivered item cannot be physically returned.
4. What is the authorised returns address? The registered office is NOT a confirmed returns address.
5. Who pays return postage and in which circumstances?
6. What is the inspection, replacement or refund procedure?

Do not reject claims automatically after 15 days. Do not add refund fees, deductions, processing deadlines or forfeiture provisions. Until confirmed, /terms publishes only the supplied damaged/non-delivery contact guidance. Its dated trading information is not represented as a complete or legally approved contract.
