## FAQ

1. **Who is the website for?** UK retailers, resellers and other trade buyers sourcing electronics and car accessories.
2. **Why can’t I see prices?** Trade prices are private. Sign in with an approved wholesale account to see any published price tiers. You can also request a tailored quotation.
3. **What is your minimum order?** Minimum quantities are confirmed per product. No unverified MOQ, pack size or minimum spend is presented as a trading promise.
4. **Can I request samples?** You can add a sample enquiry for eligible ranges. Sample availability, cost and delivery are confirmed by the team.
5. **Does checkout take payment?** No. Checkout submits a quotation request for team review. It does not confirm an order, shipping arrangement or payment.

## Content not fabricated

No testimonials, client logos, certification claims, turnover, years in business, stock numbers, discounts, free/fast shipping promises or guaranteed response times have been supplied or invented. Unknown contact details are not replaced with example phone numbers or fake addresses. Those assets can be added after verification and permission.
